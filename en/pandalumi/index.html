Pandalumi – Cozy Block Puzzle

A cozy color block puzzle with six kawaii pandas as your companions.

Coming soon to Google Play Android Game language: German

Welcome to Pandalumi – the cozy block puzzle for quick breaks and long evenings! Tap groups of three or more connected blocks of the same color, merge them into higher values and build your best block. New blocks drop in, and a little planning turns into big combos.

The game is currently available in German only.

Six pandas to collect

Mochi
Bambu
Mimi
Kumo
Kiro
Yuki

Each panda has its own personality. Unlock them, pick your favorite as a companion and watch it cheer for your combos. Tap it, tap three times for a special reaction, or pet it – it closes its eyes happily and little hearts float up.

Three game modes

  • Classic (8 × 8) – the relaxed endless mode
  • Endless XL (10 × 10) – more space and five colors
  • Level Adventure – 50 levels in five chapters with goals, obstacles and up to three stars

Evolution and wardrobe

Your companion earns experience, evolves through five stages and learns new animations. Achievements unlock accessories.

Cozy and fair

  • Play offline
  • No ads, no in-app purchases
  • No accounts, no data collection
  • Your progress and records stay on your device
