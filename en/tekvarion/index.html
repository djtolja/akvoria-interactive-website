TEKVARION

Build your aluminium factory, tune belts and machines – and earn even offline.

Coming soon to Google Play Android · landscape Game language: German

Welcome to TEKVARION – the factory tycoon for tinkerers and optimizers! You take over a small aluminium plant: the extrusion press shapes profiles, the saw cuts them, packaging gets them ready and shipping delivers them to your customers. Connect your machines with conveyor belts, splitters and switches and build a plant that runs faster and more profitably step by step.

The game is currently available in German only.

Build your factory

Place machines freely, connect them with belts, run parallel lines with splitters and switches – spot backlogs and bottlenecks at a glance.

Master the economy

Buy raw materials, expand storage, use fluctuating prices for sales, materials and electricity; keep revenue, costs and margin in view.

Research and automate

Research in five areas, production analysis with upgrade suggestions and a plant manager who can upgrade for you.

Keeps running offline

Your factory keeps producing while you are away, and a report shows what happened in the meantime.

Fair and uninterrupted

  • No ads, no in-app purchases
  • No account, no internet connection required
  • Your save stays on your device; transfer via export code
